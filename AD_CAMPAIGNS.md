# Chasr Dating — Ad Campaign Playbook

**Honest numbers first:** a dating app's cost per install (CPI) is typically $3–10. At $5/day
you're buying ~1–2 installs/day. That is enough to *test* which ad and message works, but it is
NOT enough to grow alone. So this playbook pairs **micro-budget paid ads** (testing + steady
trickle) with the **free organic channel** that actually scales dating apps.

Landing page for ALL ads (trackable):
`https://chasr-app-1.onrender.com/welcome?utm_source=PLATFORM&utm_medium=cpa&utm_campaign=CAMPAIGN`

## 1. Where to run (priority order)

| Platform | Why | Min budget | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TikTok** | Cheapest CPI for dating apps, best with UGC-style video | $5–10/day | User-generated-style video ads |
| **Meta (Instagram + Facebook)** | Biggest LGBTQ+ reach, precise interests | $5–10/day | Photo + carousel ads, retargeting |
| **Google (Search + Display)** | Low volume but high intent ("trans dating app") | $5/day | Catching people already searching |
| **Reddit ads** | Cheap, niche subreddits, very engaged | $3–5/day | Community vibe, "we built this for us" angle |

Run ONE platform at a time with the same creative, let it run 5–7 days, then judge.

## 2. Targeting (Meta / TikTok)

- **Age:** 18–34 primary, extend to 44 after testing.
- **Gender:** All (the app serves the whole community — do NOT exclude by gender).
- **Interests (Meta):** Transgender, Trans woman, LGBTQ+ community, Pride, Grindr, Her, Lex,
  Scruff, gender-neutral topics, Queer culture, Drag, RuPaul's Drag Race, LGBTQ+ rights.
- **Locations (start):** US metros with big LGBTQ+ scenes: New York, LA, SF, Chicago, Atlanta,
  Orlando, Miami, Houston, DC, Seattle, Portland, Denver, Austin, Philly, Dallas, Phoenix.
- **Exclusions:** Under-18 (mandatory), gambling, politics.
- **TikTok:** same age/gender; target US; let TikTok's algorithm find lookalikes (their
  smart-optimization does this better than manual interest lists).

## 4. Creative specs (what to run)

- **Meta:** 1080×1080 or 1080×1350; carousels of 3 images work best; keep text under 20% of image.
- **TikTok:** 9:16 video, 15–30s, vertical, subtitles on (most watch muted).
- **Google:** 300×250 + 728×90 display; search uses text only.
- **Creative direction (policy-safe):** real, warm photos of adult people in everyday settings —
  coffee dates, group hangouts, pride events. No nudity, no explicit content, no before/after
  body claims. Photos of real members with written consent convert best.
- **NOTE:** AI-generated people photos for ads are allowed on Meta/TikTok but must be disclosed
  where required, and can't imply real people/endorsements. The safest creative is photos of real
  users who consent, or staged phone-mockup screenshots of the app.
- **Always include:** the 18+ note and a link to the privacy policy in the ad.

## 6. Compliance (do NOT skip — accounts get banned)

- All dating ads: **18+ audience only**, no sexual/explicit content, no nudity, no "escort" or
  transactional language.
- No discrimination: don't exclude groups in targeting beyond age/geo.
- Privacy policy link required in ads (live at `https://chasr-app-1.onrender.com/privacy`).
- Don't claim "verified profiles" or safety features you can't back up in the ad.
- Keep screenshots/creative in line with Google/Meta adult-content policies.

## 7. The free engine that actually grows dating apps (do this FIRST)

Paid ads at $5–10/day = a trickle. This is the real growth channel until you have revenue:

- **TikTok/Reels UGC:** film 15–30s videos (your phone): "the dating app I actually feel safe
  on," "what my dating app feed looks like as a trans woman." 2–3 videos/week. This is how
  Grindr-adjacent apps got their first 10k users — authentic, not polished.
- **Reddit:** share respectfully in trans/LGBTQ+ subreddits (check each sub's self-promo rules;
  r/transdating, r/transgender, local city subs' megathreads). No spam — be a real member first.
- **Instagram/Discord:** start a Chasr page; join trans/Discord servers; answer "where do I meet
  people" questions and mention the app when it's genuinely relevant.
- **In-person:** local pride events, LGBTQ+ community centers, college groups — posters with a QR
  code to `/welcome`.
- **The invite loop you already have:** every user's one-tap invite gives both sides 7 free days
  of Chasr+. Every new user is a recruiter.
